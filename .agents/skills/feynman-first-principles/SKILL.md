# Feynman First-Principles Writing Method

A rigorous protocol for drafting deep explanatory prose that connects foundational physical or logical primitives directly to advanced phenomena without cognitive leaps, filler, or hand-waving.

## Core Pillars

### 1. The Single Continuous Thread
- **Empirical Ground Zero**: Never start from an abstract concept. Start from a tangible sensory primitive or mechanical observation that even an inquisitive child can observe directly (e.g. not "perception", but "light hitting a patch of tissue"; not "memory", but "scratch marks on wax").
- **Unbroken Derivation Chain**: Proceed in a strict linear chain where step $N+1$ is an unavoidable, immediate mechanical consequence of step $N$.
- **No Conceptual Leaps**: If moving from counting stones to differential calculus, every intermediate transition (counting $\to$ grouping $\to$ ratios $\to$ rates $\to$ instantaneous change) must be explicitly shown, not assumed.

### 2. Recursive 8-Whys Root Interrogation
For every observed behavior or mechanism, interrogate it relentlessly down to rock bottom:
1. *Why does the system do this?* (Immediate function)
2. *Why does that function require this mechanism?* (Mechanical constraint)
3. *Why can it not do it simpler?* (Trade-off or bottleneck)
4. *Why does that bottleneck exist?* (Physical / energetic limitation)
5. *Why did evolution or physics converge on this solution?* (Optimization landscape)
6. *Why does this failure mode occur?* (Boundary condition)
7. *Why does the subjective experience feel like something else?* (Phenomenological gap)
8. *What fundamental law of information or matter underpins it?* (Irreducible axiom)

### 3. Absolute Incompressibility & Anti-Fluff
- **Zero Filler**: Eliminate academic hedging ("It is worth noting that...", "Studies suggest that...", "In many respects...").
- **Information Density**: Every sentence must provide a causal explanation, an exact constraint, or an observable reality.
- **Candid Precision**: Call illusions illusions, errors errors, and limits limits. Write with visceral clarity and intellectual honesty.
- **Concrete Metaphors**: Use physical, tangible analogies (gears, pulleys, water tanks, telegraph wires, thermodynamic engines) that ground abstraction in physical intuition.

## Chapter Architecture Standard
1. **The Ground Anchor**: Opening paragraph establishing the foundational observation and mystery.
2. **The 8-Whys Descent**: The recursive drilling down to the irreducible constraint.
3. **The Mechanical Ascent**: Building the complex architectural phenomenon up from the irreducible constraint.
4. **The Causal Linchpin**: A pithy, definitive pull quote capturing the core physical law.
5. **The Structural Reality**: The operational consequences for mind, decision, and reality.
